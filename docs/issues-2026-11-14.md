# Stabilisation and bug-fix round, 2026-11-14: issue list

Found before fixing, from the new `validate maps liquids abuse extremes render` checks, the
full gallery (`sprout gallery`), screenshots with the panel, `probe`, and reading the code.
Status is filled in as each is dealt with; measurements are in README, Validation, "Bug-fix
round".

## Solver

| # | What | How it was found | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Ferrofluid map collapses within 1 s: the dish is crushed into a sub-cell film on the floor, particles race at the speed cap (2377 cells/s), one cell holds 9045 particles (the fixed-point sums wrap). The magnetic force was applied per particle, sampled between grid points: not a discrete gradient, so the pressure solve could not balance it (~75 g near the magnet). | `validate maps`, `probe --map 17`, gallery `ferrofluid_spikes` | fixed |
| S2 | A solid laid over a full pool (a lid) squeezes all ~4000 displaced particles into one cell: the sealed region's vent is the only place the density-correction potential is held, so every surplus particle drains into it. | `validate abuse` | fixed |
| S3 | A current aimed at the floor or a corner packs 75 particles into a cell (it is not bounded by `tool_crowd_max` as Push and Grab are). | `validate abuse` | fixed |
| S4 | Slime dropped into a cup shatters into a mist of single droplets (with `shear_modulus` 0 it splashes into ropes): sparse spray particles keep their elastic stress and its divergence acts on them. | gallery `liquid_slime` | partly fixed: detached droplets drop their stress (fewer, larger droplets); the rest is the impact itself (1 Pa s dropped 40 cm splashes without elasticity too). Restricting the elastic force to faces between liquid cells broke the validated shear mode, so it was not kept |
| S5 | Particles at the speed cap in liquid at rest: sludge in a cup after 8 s, Mud Flats, Cold Snap, Lava Meets Water (fastest 752 cells/s = the cap while rms is a few cells/s). Cause: the midpoint rule cancels a surface spray particle's motion while its gathered velocity banks gravity. | `validate liquids`, `validate maps` | fixed (sludge 752 → 17 cells/s) |
| S6 | Lava under water grows upward "coral" fingers with water inclusions instead of lying on the floor under a crust; the fingers glow. Measured: they are molten (5 – 15 % frozen); boiling leaves voids that the pressure solve treats as free surfaces, which implode and draw the lava up. | gallery `lava_water` | open: needs a vapour phase |
| S7 | Density Tower after 12 s: oil and wax (one phase group, different densities) stay a mottled patchwork; neither separates nor mixes. Water specks stay suspended in the oil. Cause: no drift inside a phase group, no mixing without a diluent; at 20 °C air the wax also sets into chunks as dense as the oil. Warm air did not remove the mottle. | gallery `density_tower` | open: needs a temperature-free per-material density on the grid |
| S8 | A falling stream hitting the floor packs 20–50 particles per cell in the spreading sheet for a moment (fresh tank too). Transient, not a runaway. | `validate abuse` | note (Limitations) |
| S9 | Particles stuck inside solids: 1.3 cells inside Canopy's crown of two wedges, 18 cells inside a Terrace Falls block (seen in intermediate runs). The minimum of the shapes' distances has internal seams; the push beyond the surface can cross a narrow gap into the next solid. | `validate maps` | fixed (`Scene::union_depth`, surface-only rescue push) |
| S10 | Two boxes 2 cells apart: the gap between them packs 50 – 90 particles per cell for about 1.5 s after one is laid over liquid (old build too). | `validate abuse` | note: transient |
| T1 | "After the stir: energy above rest falls" compares single samples 2 s apart against a ~2 s slosh: fails under 0.1 % parameter changes (1 of 8 runs before, up to 4 of 8 on intermediate builds). | nudged runs of `validate settle` | reworked as the Grab check was (2 s means, rise ≤ the correction's lift): 16 of 16 pass |
